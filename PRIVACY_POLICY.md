# Privacy Policy for AnemiaScan

**Effective date:** 22 September 2026

AnemiaScan is a mobile visual-screening support app for anemia and jaundice. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information handled by the app

AnemiaScan may access the device camera or photo library only after permission is granted by the user. These images are used on the device to perform the selected visual-screening analysis.

The app may retain locally stored screening history, including the selected screening mode, date, and displayed screening result, so the user can view prior activity within the app.

## No data collection or sharing

AnemiaScan does not operate a backend service, require an account, upload photos, sell personal information, or share images or screening-history data with the developer or third parties. Image analysis and local history storage take place on the user's device.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** used to capture a photo when the user chooses live capture.
- **Photos / media:** used only when the user chooses an existing photo for analysis.

Permissions can be changed at any time through the device settings.

## Medical disclaimer

AnemiaScan is not a diagnostic device. It does not measure haemoglobin or bilirubin, and its screening results are not medical diagnoses, disease probabilities, or a substitute for professional medical advice, laboratory testing, diagnosis, or treatment. Seek qualified medical care for health concerns or urgent symptoms.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because data is handled locally and not transmitted to the developer, the app does not maintain a user database.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page.
